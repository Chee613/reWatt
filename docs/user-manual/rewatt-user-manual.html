Step 1

Prepare your old phone

  1. Charge it.Charge the phone fully before you start.
  2. Clear it.Back up your old photos. Then reset the phone or delete them.
  3. Connect to Wi-Fi.Use the Wi-Fi of the room it will watch.
  4. Update it.Install any Android and app updates.
  5. Allow the install.Settings → Apps → Special access → Install unknown apps. Allow your browser or file app.
Step 2

Install the app

  1. Download.Get the reWatt app file (.apk) from the link that came with your kit.
  2. Install.Tap the file, then tap Install.
  3. Open.Tap Open. The Quick Setup Guide starts by itself.
TipAndroid may warn about unknown apps. This is normal for apps not from the Play Store.

Connect the module

A B C D E
  • A
    reWatt moduleSits behind the phone.

    Phone's charging portThe 180° adapter plugs in here.

  1. Clip it on.Place the module on the back of the phone. Use the magnet or adhesive pad.
  2. Plug into the phone.Push the Type-C 180° adapter into the phone's charging port (B) and the module port (C).

Using reWatt

Your readings explained

Reading What it means
Temperature How warm the room is, in °C. Measured by the SHT31.
Humidity How damp the air is, in %. About 40–60% feels comfortable.
Occupancy How many people the camera counts. The radar wakes the camera when someone is there.
Lights · Fan · AC Shows if each appliance is on (green) or off (red).
VOC Index Gases from overheating wires or a gas leak. Normal is about 100.
NOx Index Gases from something burning. Normal is about 1.

How reWatt saves energy

  • People in the room → reWatt picks the right AC setting for the headcount.
  • More people in the room → reWatt lowers the AC temperature to keep everyone cool.
  • No one for 5 minutes → the room's lights, fan and AC switch off.
  • Empty room → camera and AI stay off to save power.
Good to knowYou can also see these readings on another device. See Connect to MQTT, page 13.

When does the alarm ring?
Normal airVOC 130+ or NOx 5+ for 2 seconds → ALARM
If the alarm ringsGet everyone out. Call 994 (Bomba). Only tap DISMISS when you are safe.

The SECURITY screen

Settings → FAMILY faces
Armed: a stranger is seen
Siren + intruder alert
  1. Add familySettings → FAMILY faces. Add a few photos of each person.
  2. Arm itOpen SECURITY. Turn on ARMED when you leave or at night.
  3. Family?reWatt stays silent.
  1. Stranger?It says "You are being recorded". Then the siren, flashing light and alert start.
  2. Stop itTap DISMISS.
Private by designFaces are checked on the phone. Nothing is uploaded. Turn off ARMED to add or change faces. Past alerts are listed below the camera.

Help

Quick fixes

Problem Try this
OFFLINE Check the adapter is pushed in. Unplug and plug it back in.
No live view Allow the camera permission. Clean the lens.
App stops at night Turn off Battery Saver for reWatt (setup page 2).
Alarm is silent Allow all security permissions (setup page 3).
Wrong headcount Move the phone so the whole room is in view.
Family member alerted Add more photos of that person in FAMILY faces.

Care & safety

  • Use indoors only. Keep it dry.
  • Do not cover the module's vents or the camera.
  • Use a good-quality charger and cable.
  • Do not open the module or bend its parts.
  • reWatt helps you stay safe. It does not replace a certified smoke detector.
